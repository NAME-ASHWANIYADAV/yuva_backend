# DEMO_SCRIPT (5 minutes)

Before starting: backend running (give it about a minute after start: the named scenarios are precomputed), browser at http://127.0.0.1:8000/demo, day 2025-10-20 (default; a rabi day with chana and wheat in the ground). Say once at the start: "Everything on screen is computed live by the backend; the feeder is modelled from MSEDCL norms, the weather and forecasts are real archives."

| Time | Click | What the audience sees | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00 | **Replay published timetable** | Grey dashed load line and grey Gantt bars = MSEDCL's Annexure-A slots. PT-2 line near 99% when Lamjana II starts at 09:30 while Jawali and Chalburga are on. Verifier line shows whether today's practice passes the pessimistic check. | "This is the timetable MSEDCL publishes for Lamjana. Three feeders on one 5 MVA transformer overlap for six and a half hours." |
| 00:45 | **Certify SUNFLOW plan** | Status badge CERTIFIED. Green Gantt bars shift; metrics strip shows import, solar used, PT-2 peak, max hot-spot, ageing vs published. "Why this plan" lists the binding constraints. | "Same eight hours per feeder. The optimiser moved the starts so PT-2 never crosses its rating and the hottest transformers stay under the IEC limit, under pessimistic assumptions, and an independent verifier confirmed it." |
| 01:30 | **Cloud ramp** | Solar band collapses mid-afternoon (a real ERA5 day, the worst midday ramp of 2025). Plan re-solves; import counter rises honestly; badge stays CERTIFIED. Tick "intra-day" first to show locked blocks before 11:00. | "This is the worst real cloud day of 2025. The plan moves what it can; what it cannot save shows up as import, not as a hidden number." |
| 02:00 | **Heat wave** (hottest real 2025 day + 3 °C) | Badge CERTIFIED, rung `baseline_guard`. "Why" says: 182 of 591 candidate schedules per feeder were excluded because 100 kVA transformers would pass the planning limit; the longest admissible single spell is 6.8 h, so every optimiser plan needs split spells or night compensation and costs more; the published timetable still passes the independent verifier (under 120 °C) and is cheaper, so it is issued unchanged. | "On the hottest day plus three degrees the optimiser cannot find a cheaper plan that respects its safety margin, and it says so: it refuses to change the timetable when the change costs money and buys no safety. That refusal is the product." |
| 02:15 | **Fail a transformer** | One Chalburga DT tile turns grey. Its pumps are re-tapped to the two neighbours only up to their headroom; the rest (11 pumps) are reported as unserved, not hidden. | 02:35 | **Fail feeder Kharosa** (11:00–13:00) | Badge CERTIFIED WITH RELAXATION, rung split_spells (about 9 s). Kharosa gets 07:30–11:00 + 13:00–17:30, still 8 h; the published slot would deliver only 6 h, so the published timetable fails the pessimistic check (supply hours). Import rises because the full 8 h are delivered. | "A two-hour outage. One contiguous spell cannot deliver eight hours any more, so the ladder allows the one relaxation the circular allows: two spells. Import goes up because the farmers get their full eight hours." |
| 02:50 | **Everyone switches on** | Badge CERTIFIED WITH RELAXATION (about 18 s): at participation 1.0 the three PT-2 feeders cannot overlap, so the plan uses split spells and the circular's night-compensation clause; the published timetable overloads PT-2 (106 %). The only rule-compliant plan needs night compensation, and the import figure includes those night hours honestly." |
| 03:10 | **Farmer request** Chalburga @ 07:30 on the base day (granted in about 5 s, cost delta shown), then Jawali @ 16:00 (refused at once: operating window, nearest feasible start shown), then with **Everyone switches on** active, Jawali @ 09:30 (refused: power-transformer rating and switching cap; nearest feasible start found with split spells; about 30 s because every contrastive solve is a split-spell model) | Decision card: GRANTED or REFUSED with the rule family, the PT numbers, the nearest feasible alternative when one exists, and the Marathi message. | "A farmer presses 1 on the IVR and asks for a slot. The answer is yes, or no with the exact rule and the nearest slot that works." |
| 03:45 | **Impossible case** | Badge INFEASIBLE. Alert says no plan satisfies every hard rule; conflict attribution lists which single rule family would restore feasibility; the published timetable is shown as the fallback. | "Ask for the impossible and it says so. It never invents a plan; it falls back to today's timetable with an alert." |
| 04:30 | **Show impact** | Table of median (P10–P90) per-day metrics over real weather days: published vs ablation variants vs full; ablation shows what each component adds. | "Every number here is modelled, labelled, and reproducible with one script." |
| 05:00 | Close on the footer | Honesty footer. | "The physics and the optimiser are real; the load is modelled; the field test is at the feeder meters that already exist." |

Recovery: if any action errors, click **Reset day**. Timings on the development laptop: certify and nominal what-ifs under 1 s; **Fail feeder** about 9 s (split spells); **Everyone switches on** about 18 s (split spells + night compensation, verified at participation 1.0); **Impossible case** about 17 s including conflict attribution. Precomputed scenarios return instantly and the log says 'cached' with the original compute time.
