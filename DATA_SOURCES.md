Labels: **PUBLIC** (downloaded from a public source), **MODELLED** (computed by our physics/ML from public inputs), **ASSUMED** (our stated assumption with a range), **SYNTHETIC** (generated by us from the assumptions). Nothing here is measured at Lamjana; no MSEDCL telemetry is public.

## What is NOT available (and what we did instead)
- Feeder-level 15-minute plant output, DT loading, DT temperatures, pump inventory, feeder-meter energy, farmer behaviour: not public. We synthesise them from the published rules and the assumptions above and label every derived number MODELLED/ASSUMED.
- Satellite 15-minute irradiance (Meteosat-IODC via Open-Meteo) returned no data for the probed 2025 dates and is not used.
- The only ground-truth-like quantities in the system are the published rules/topology and the archived weather/forecast records.

## Reproduce
```
python scripts/fetch_data.py        # ~8 MB into data/raw (cached CSV, idempotent)
python scripts/train_forecast.py    # models/forecast_*.txt, models/forecast_meta.json, results/forecast/*
```
